Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy explains what information TypeDle ("the site", "we") collects when you play, why, and what choices you have. TypeDle is a hobby project operated by an individual, not a company, but we still want to be clear and upfront about data handling.

Information we collect

Gameplay data stored in your browser

To keep the game working day to day, TypeDle stores small pieces of data directly in your browser using cookies and local storage:

  • Your current in-progress guesses, wrong-guess count, and result for the day's puzzle.
  • A cached copy of global stats (guess distribution, win rate) so the results screen loads quickly.
  • A randomly generated guest ID, so repeated results from the same browser aren't counted twice in global stats.
  • If you're signed in, a record of which account is currently logged in on this device.

None of this is used to identify you personally or track you across other websites.

Account data

Creating an account is optional. If you do, we store the username you choose, a cryptographically hashed version of your password (we never store or see your actual password), your streak, your stats, and your history of completed daily puzzles. This lets your progress sync across devices when you log in.

Standard server logs

Like almost every website, our hosting providers (GitHub Pages for the site, Render for the stats/account API) automatically log basic technical information for security and reliability, such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps. We don't separately collect or analyze this data ourselves.

Third-party services

  • PokeAPI (pokeapi.co): your browser fetches public Pokémon data (sprites, evolution info) directly from this service to render clues. No personal data is sent as part of these requests.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google's font CDN, which may log standard request data (such as IP address) the same way any web font request does.
  • Google AdSense: TypeDle may display ads served by Google. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data at google.com/policies/technologies/partner-sites, and manage your ad personalization settings at adssettings.google.com.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. Doing so may reset your in-progress puzzle or require you to log in again, but won't prevent you from playing. If ads are shown on this site, you can opt out of personalized advertising for participating providers via optout.aboutads.info or your Google Ad Settings linked above.

Children's privacy

TypeDle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account or provided information to us, please contact us using the details below and we'll remove it.

Data retention and your rights

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, so contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
