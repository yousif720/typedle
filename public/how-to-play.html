Guide

How to play TypeDle

TypeDle is a daily Pokémon guessing game. There's one secret Pokémon per day, shared by everyone who plays. You get 6 guesses to figure out who it is, and each wrong guess reveals another clue about the answer's type matchups, origin, and traits.

The goal

Type a Pokémon's name into the guess box and submit it. If you're right, you win and the round ends immediately. If you're wrong, the guess is recorded, a new clue row is revealed, and you get another try, for up to 6 guesses total per day.

The clue rows

The "Weak to" row is visible from the start. Every wrong guess reveals one more row, in this order:

  • Weak toAlways visible. Types that deal super-effective damage to the answer, split into a 4x row and a 2x row.
  • RegionRevealed after guess 1. The Pokédex region the answer originally comes from (e.g. Kanto, Galar, Paldea).
  • Evolution stageRevealed after guess 2. Whether the answer is a first-stage, middle-stage, final-stage, Mega, or non-evolving Pokémon.
  • ResistsRevealed after guess 3. Types that deal reduced damage to the answer (1/2x, then 1/4x), plus any types the answer is fully immune to (0x).
  • AbilityRevealed after guess 4. One of the answer's abilities.

Guesses 5 and 6 don't reveal anything new; they're your last chances to use everything you've already seen to lock in the answer.

Reading your own guesses

Every guess you make is listed under the board with a small chip showing how much of its typing overlaps with the answer. A guess that shares both types with a dual-type answer lights up green; one that shares a single type turns amber; no overlap stays grey. It's partial credit, so a near miss still tells you something useful about what you're hunting.

Other ways to play

  • PracticeUnlimited random rounds that never touch your streak or stats. Play as many as you like while you wait for tomorrow's puzzle.
  • CustomizeBuild your own practice pool: pick the regions you want, and choose whether Mega and Primal forms are included, excluded, or the only thing you face.
  • Create-a-dlePick any Pokémon and get a link that turns it into a puzzle for whoever opens it. The name is encoded in the link so it isn't spoiled at a glance.
  • RewindReplay any earlier day from the calendar button next to the guess box.
  • Hard modeTurn it on in Settings to freeze the clue board after the second wrong guess. The last rows never unlock.

Sharing your result

Finishing a round gives you a spoiler-free emoji grid: one square per guess, green only on the winning one. It never contains the answer's name, so you can post it anywhere without ruining the day for anyone.

Streaks and stats

Solve the daily puzzle to keep your streak going. Miss a day or run out of guesses and it resets. Your best streak is saved so you can track your progress over time. Every result also contributes to a global guess-distribution chart, so you can see how everyone else did on the same puzzle.

Accounts and history

Playing as a guest works fine: your in-progress round is kept on your own device, so a refresh won't lose it. Creating an account adds the parts a single device can't do, syncing your streak, stats, and completed days everywhere you play, and putting you on the leaderboard. You can also add friends by username to see a leaderboard of just the people you know.

Installing it as an app

TypeDle can be installed to your home screen or desktop from your browser's menu. It then opens in its own window, keeps working when you're offline, and behaves like any other app on your device.

Accessibility

Settings has a light, dark, or system theme, a high-contrast mode, a reduced-motion mode that turns off flips and other animations, and a toggle for sound effects. The board is keyboard navigable throughout: Enter submits a guess, the arrow keys move through suggestions, Esc closes a dialog, and ? opens the in-game help.

Tip

Start with a Pokémon whose typing you're confident about. The "Weak to" row alone often narrows things down to a handful of type combinations before you've used a single guess.
